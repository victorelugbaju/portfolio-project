02 — About Across consumer and enterprise

I don't mind complicated.

Since 2024 I've worked at both ends of the same job. On one side, consumer products where everything turns on whether someone can finish a task without stopping to think. On the other, internal systems where four roles see four views of the same record and none of them can be wrong. I was team lead on one of those, covering 110+ screens and 21 flows. The domains have been finance software, consumer health and AI, and the tooling a business runs on.

What carries across is the order I work in. I find out what the real problem is, map the flow before I draw a screen, then design the system underneath it: the components, states and rules that keep a product coherent as it grows. I prototype early, because some problems only appear once you can click through. And because I can read the frontend myself, I meet constraints while they are still cheap to design around, instead of at handoff.

03 — Principles How I shape products
01

Clarity over cleverness

I design for understanding first. If a user has to think too hard about what to do next, the design has already failed.

02

Systems, not screens

I don't just design individual screens — I design systems that scale. This keeps products cohesive as they grow.

03

Real problems, practical solutions

I focus on solving the problems that actually matter — driven by user needs, context and business goals, not trends or assumptions.

04

Bridge between design and engineering

I design with implementation in mind. A working knowledge of frontend development means my designs are not just visually strong but efficient to build.
